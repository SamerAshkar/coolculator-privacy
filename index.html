Privacy Policy

Last Updated: Aug 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Coolculator Escape (the "Game"), created by Samer Ashkar, handles your information. I am committed to ensuring that your player privacy is protected while you enjoy the Game.

Privacy Commitment: Coolculator Escape is designed to be a private gaming experience. We do not collect real-world identity data (like names or emails) directly, and we never sell your gameplay progress to third parties.

1. Gameplay Data & Usage

The Game does not collect personally identifiable information (PII). To provide a rewarding experience and save your accomplishments, the Game manages the following Gameplay Data:

  • High Scores: Your best run distances and calculator game records.
  • Progression: Total Experience Points (XP) and current player level.
  • Virtual Economy: Your total collected Coins used for in-game purchases.
  • Inventory: Information on which Skins, Characters, Cities, and Keychains you have unlocked or equipped.
  • Settings: Your preferred sound volume and graphics quality settings.

2. Storage & Progress Syncing

To ensure your progress is never lost, the Game uses three methods of storage:

  • Local Storage: Your progress is saved directly to your device so you can play without an active connection.
  • Cloud Sync (Firebase): The Game uses Google Firebase to back up your progress. This uses Anonymous Authentication; while a unique ID is assigned to your save file, it is not linked to your identity or contact details.
  • Cloud Save (Google Play Games): The Game uses Google Play Games Services "Snapshots" to securely store your progress in the cloud. This data is linked to your Google Play Games profile, allowing you to restore your accomplishments even after reinstalling the Game or switching devices.

3. Audio & Permissions

The Game generates all sound effects and music locally using the Web Audio API. It does not require or request access to your device's microphone, camera, contacts, or photo gallery.

4. Third-Party Services

We use Google Firebase (Firestore and Auth) and Google Play Games Services exclusively for save-game hosting and progress synchronization. We do not use third-party advertising networks, analytics trackers, or social media plugins that monitor your behavior outside of the Game.

5. Children’s Privacy

Coolculator Escape is intended for players of all ages. Because the Game does not collect names, locations, or contact info, it is compliant with standard privacy regulations regarding children and does not solicit data from users under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect new game features. Any changes will be reflected on this page with a new "Last Updated" date.
